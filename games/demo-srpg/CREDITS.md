To rebuild the style: download the packs, then
`python3 tools/art/make_detailed_style.py --kaykit <folder with Knight.glb, Barbarian.glb, Mage.glb, Rogue_Hooded.glb> --animals <folder with Horse.glb> --weapons <folder with Spear.glb and Bow.glb (the Wooden Bow)> --textures <folder with the *_Color.jpg files>`.
Combining (a spear or bow on a hand joint, a rider seated on the horse's body joint, one clip moving both skeletons) is done by
`tools/art/make_detailed_units.py`; the Quaternius models are saved from the pages above as `.glb` (FBX2glTF export) and renamed to the file names shown.
